# Surface brief: site/index.html (landing page)

Mode: Persuade. Build path: code-led (no image generation available).

Audience: students and researchers writing theses in Typst on Windows. Job: decide whether to install. Action: download the Windows installer (GitHub release v0.1.0); secondary: view the source on GitHub. Proof: the real app screenshot and a working highlight-and-note demo. Constraints: no invented users, numbers or claims; installer is unsigned and says so.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a library-bound thesis, one book seen once: closed on arrival, then opened below into its own pages. It refuses the category default of a dark hero, screenshot and feature-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Bottle-green buckram cloth with woven grain, flat gold-foil lettering, a spine with gilt double rules and a white library spine label. Inside: bright white bond paper, black Libertinus Serif (Typst's own default face) with small caps, running heads, page numbers, dot-leader contents, numbered figures and tables, footnotes. Highlighter yellow, green, blue and pink appear only where the comment feature does.

STORY: The visitor reads the cover and knows this is a Typst editor for theses; the contents page is the navigation; the chapters show live preview, two-way jump, comments and export; they download from the spine label or the final chapter.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The closed book fills the viewport: the spine on the left carries vertical foil lettering and the spine label, which is the primary action "Download for Windows". The front board carries the gold title, a one-line description and "by Kong".

FORM: Bound thesis, candidate 3 of 7. seed: none (roll skipped). The impeccable launcher (and its concept-seed roll) was not run; the lead "Conference poster" was dealt by a local random roll from the top three candidates, and the user then chose Bound thesis over it. Disclosed to the user.

Signature interaction: Figure 3.1 is a live typeset paragraph that visitors drag-highlight in one of four colors and pin a sticky note to. Motion: the front board swings open on the spine as the visitor scrolls past the cover (plays on desktop even under reduced motion, by the user's choice; off at 720px and below).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
